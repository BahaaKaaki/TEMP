# Customer / commercial strategy

## When to use this skill
Use for revenue acceleration, commercial turnarounds, segment strategies, sales model redesign, and customer-led growth agendas. Do not use for a pure product strategy or standalone pricing study.

## Executive summary
One page only. It should track the full deck and usually contain 3–5 messages around target customers, commercial choices, growth levers, value impact, and execution direction.

## Typical output
Typical length: 10–16 pages in a standard version. This is the default structure for a broad ask; narrower requests may cover only one section or page, while deeper asks may extend the deck.

### 0) Executive summary — 1 page
**What it is:** One-page commercial storyline tracker  
**What it includes:** answer, 3–5 messages, growth implication, execution direction  
**Typical formats:** message page, commercial summary, pillar page

### 1) Commercial baseline — 1–3 pages
**What it is:** Current customer and commercial performance  
**What it includes:** revenue mix, customer mix, current channels, pain points, baseline economics  
**Typical formats:** performance summary, funnel, customer mix

### 2) Customer and segment perspective — 1–3 pages
**What it is:** Who matters most and why  
**What it includes:** segments, needs, behaviors, profitability, strategic priority  
**Typical formats:** segmentation, journey map, segment heatmap

### 3) Commercial choices — 1–3 pages
**What it is:** The core choices to improve growth  
**What it includes:** target customers, proposition, channels, pricing posture, sales focus  
**Typical formats:** 2x2, choice matrix, commercial model

### 4) Growth levers and initiatives — 2–4 pages
**What it is:** How growth will actually be delivered  
**What it includes:** segment plays, sales levers, channel moves, pricing / offer moves, capability needs  
**Typical formats:** initiative tree, lever page, portfolio heatmap

### 5) Roadmap and economics — 1–3 pages
**What it is:** How the commercial agenda will land  
**What it includes:** sequencing, value logic, owners, dependencies  
**Typical formats:** roadmap, value bridge, phased plan

### 6) KPIs and governance — 1–2 pages
**What it is:** How commercial delivery will be tracked  
**What it includes:** KPIs, review cadence, ownership, escalation  
**Typical formats:** KPI scorecard, governance chart

## How to build
- diagnose current commercial performance
- segment customers and economics
- define the key commercial choices
- translate choices into practical levers and initiatives
- sequence the moves and quantify impact
- close with KPIs and governance

## Success criteria
- customer view drives the answer
- commercial choices are explicit
- levers connect to growth impact
- execution logic is visible
- deck feels practical, not conceptual

## Common failure modes
- too broad and unfocused
- customer insights disconnected from actions
- commercial levers listed, not prioritized
- no economic logic
- no execution model
