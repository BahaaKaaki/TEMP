# Digital / technology strategy

## When to use this skill
Use for enterprise digital strategy, technology-enabled transformation, data and AI agenda, digital priorities, or aligning tech investments to business outcomes. Do not use for a detailed IT architecture or system implementation design.

## Executive summary
**One page only.**  
It should track the full deck and usually contain 3–5 components such as digital ambition, baseline and gaps, priority use cases, enablers, and implementation direction.

## Typical output
Typical length: 10–16 pages in a standard version. This is the default for a broad ask; narrower requests may cover only one section or page, while deeper asks may extend the deck.

### 0) Executive summary — 1 page
**What it is:** One-page storyline tracker for the digital deck  
**What it includes:** digital answer, 3–5 messages, value implication, execution direction  
**Typical formats:** message page; pillar summary; digital strategy house

### 1) Business context and digital ambition — 1–2 pages
**What it is:** Why digital matters for the business  
**What it includes:** business priorities, digital ambition, strategic context  
**Typical formats:** context page; ambition page; strategy house

### 2) Digital baseline and pain points — 1–3 pages
**What it is:** Current state of digital, data, and technology  
**What it includes:** maturity, pain points, channel and process issues, platform gaps  
**Typical formats:** maturity heatmap; current-state snapshot; issue tree

### 3) Outside-in perspective — 1–3 pages
**What it is:** External digital, customer, and technology trends  
**What it includes:** benchmark cases, tech shifts, customer expectations, competitor moves  
**Typical formats:** benchmark table; trend summary; market map

### 4) Priority themes and use cases — 2–4 pages
**What it is:** What the digital strategy should focus on  
**What it includes:** priority themes, use cases, value pools, trade-offs  
**Typical formats:** use-case portfolio; 2x2; initiative tree

### 5) Enablers and target model — 1–3 pages
**What it is:** What must change to deliver the strategy  
**What it includes:** data, platforms, operating model, talent, governance, partnerships  
**Typical formats:** target-state view; capability model; operating model page

### 6) Roadmap and investment logic — 1–2 pages
**What it is:** How the strategy will be sequenced  
**What it includes:** waves, milestones, dependencies, investment priorities  
**Typical formats:** roadmap; phased plan; chevron with sub-steps

### 7) KPIs and governance — 1–2 pages
**What it is:** How delivery and value will be tracked  
**What it includes:** digital KPIs, governance, review cadence  
**Typical formats:** KPI scorecard; governance chart; cadence view

## How to build
- define the business question the digital strategy must solve
- assess current digital maturity, platforms, pain points, and opportunity areas
- bring an outside-in view on market, customer, and technology trends
- define priority digital themes, use cases, and capability shifts
- translate into roadmap, enablers, and governance

## Typical data needed
business strategy and priorities; current systems and platforms; customer and channel data; digital maturity inputs; process pain points; benchmark practices; data / AI opportunities; investment constraints

## Success criteria
Digital is linked to business value; use cases are prioritized; enablers are explicit; roadmap is realistic; governance is clear.

## Common failure modes
Tech shopping list; no business linkage; too many use cases; no operating or data enablers; implementation path too vague.
