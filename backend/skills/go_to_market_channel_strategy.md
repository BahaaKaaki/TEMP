# Go-to-market / channel strategy

## When to use this skill
Use for launches, expansion, channel redesign, GTM refresh, sales coverage strategy, or commercial activation plans. Do not use for a broad enterprise strategy without a clear route-to-market question.

## Executive summary
One page only. It should track the full deck and usually contain 3–5 messages around target customers, channel model, sales approach, activation priorities, and growth implication.

## Typical output
Typical length: 8–12 pages in a standard version. This is the default structure for a broad ask; narrower requests may cover only one section or page, while deeper asks may extend the deck.

### 0) Executive summary — 1 page
**What it is:** One-page GTM storyline tracker  
**What it includes:** answer, 3–5 messages, growth implication, activation direction  
**Typical formats:** message page, GTM summary, recommendation page

### 1) Growth objective and target market — 1–2 pages
**What it is:** What growth the GTM must unlock  
**What it includes:** target customers, markets, segments, ambition, constraints  
**Typical formats:** objective page, market lens, segment summary

### 2) Buyer journey and channel baseline — 1–2 pages
**What it is:** How customers buy today and where channels matter  
**What it includes:** journey, touchpoints, current channels, performance, friction points  
**Typical formats:** journey map, funnel, channel map

### 3) GTM choices — 1–2 pages
**What it is:** The core route-to-market decisions  
**What it includes:** direct vs indirect, channel roles, sales motion, coverage approach  
**Typical formats:** channel matrix, archetype page, decision grid

### 4) Sales and channel model — 1–3 pages
**What it is:** How the commercial machine will work  
**What it includes:** channel coverage, account model, partner model, enablement, incentives  
**Typical formats:** coverage model, segment-channel matrix, sales model

### 5) Activation roadmap — 1–2 pages
**What it is:** How GTM is launched and scaled  
**What it includes:** waves, milestones, capability needs, launch priorities  
**Typical formats:** roadmap, phased plan, chevron

### 6) KPIs and governance — 1 page
**What it is:** How GTM delivery is tracked  
**What it includes:** conversion, productivity, channel mix, cadence, ownership  
**Typical formats:** KPI scorecard, governance chart

## How to build
- define the growth objective and target customers
- map the buyer journey and current route-to-market
- make the core GTM choices
- design sales and channel coverage
- define activation priorities and milestones
- close with KPIs and governance

## Success criteria
- target customer and channel logic are aligned
- GTM choices are explicit
- sales and partner roles are clear
- activation is practical
- KPIs track channel performance

## Common failure modes
- channel list without strategy
- no link to customer journey
- sales model too vague
- roadmap lacks activation logic
- no ownership or KPIs
