# Kick-off / mobilization pack

## When to use this skill
Use at the start of a project, workstream, PMO, or transformation to launch properly and avoid ambiguity in the first weeks. Typical asks include kick-off decks, inception packs, mobilization plans, first-meeting materials, and initial ways-of-working setup. Do not use for midstream status reporting or for a final readout.

## Executive summary
**One page only.**  
It should track the full deck and usually contain 3–5 components such as project objective, scope and workplan, governance, immediate asks, and first milestones.

## Typical output
Typical length: 6–10 pages in a standard version. This is the default structure for a broad ask; narrower requests may cover only one section or page, while deeper asks may extend the deck.

### 0) Executive summary — 1 page
**What it is:** One-page storyline tracker for the mobilization pack  
**What it includes:** objective, scope, workplan, governance, immediate asks  
**Typical formats:** message page; launch summary; kickoff overview

### 1) Objective, scope, and outcomes — 1–2 pages
**What it is:** What the project is trying to achieve and what is included  
**What it includes:** objective, scope, exclusions, expected outcomes, success criteria  
**Typical formats:** scope box; objective page; outcomes page

### 2) Ways of working and roles — 1–2 pages
**What it is:** How the team and client will work together  
**What it includes:** client and advisor roles, working norms, review cycles, collaboration principles  
**Typical formats:** RACI; roles page; ways-of-working page

### 3) Workplan and milestone calendar — 1–2 pages
**What it is:** How the first phases of work will unfold  
**What it includes:** phases, key activities, milestones, workshops, decision points  
**Typical formats:** chevron; workplan; milestone calendar

### 4) Governance and forums — 1–2 pages
**What it is:** How decisions and alignment will be managed  
**What it includes:** governance bodies, forum cadence, escalation path, approvals  
**Typical formats:** governance map; forum calendar; escalation flow

### 5) Immediate asks and dependencies — 1–2 pages
**What it is:** What is needed immediately to start well  
**What it includes:** initial document or data asks, stakeholder access, dependencies, assumptions, risks  
**Typical formats:** asks tracker; dependency list; risk box

### 6) Kick-off agenda and next steps — 1–2 pages
**What it is:** How the launch session and immediate follow-up will run  
**What it includes:** meeting agenda, objectives by session, post-kickoff actions, owners and dates  
**Typical formats:** agenda page; action tracker; next-step calendar

## How to build
- restate the objective, scope, and intended outcomes in plain terms
- align on what is in and out and how success will be judged
- translate the work into an initial phased plan and milestone view
- clarify roles, ways of working, and governance from day one
- capture immediate asks, dependencies, and initial decisions
- prepare the launch agenda and the next-step actions after kick-off

## Success criteria
The client and team leave the kick-off aligned on scope, first steps, ownership, and timing, with no ambiguity about what is needed immediately.

## Common failure modes
Kick-off is generic; scope remains fuzzy; immediate asks are buried; no clear owners or milestones; ways of working are assumed rather than agreed.
