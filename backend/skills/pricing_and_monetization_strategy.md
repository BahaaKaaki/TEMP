# Pricing and monetization strategy

## When to use this skill
Use for price redesign, monetization strategy, packaging and tiering, pricing transformation, or launching new revenue models. Do not use for a full customer strategy without a pricing focus.

## Executive summary
One page only. It should track the full deck and usually contain 3–5 messages around pricing objective, pricing architecture, monetization model, economics, and rollout implication.

## Typical output
Typical length: 8–12 pages in a standard version. This is the default structure for a broad ask; narrower requests may cover only one section or page, while deeper asks may extend the deck.

### 0) Executive summary — 1 page
**What it is:** One-page pricing storyline tracker  
**What it includes:** answer, 3–5 messages, economic implication, rollout direction  
**Typical formats:** message page, pricing summary, recommendation page

### 1) Pricing context and objective — 1 page
**What it is:** Why pricing is being revisited  
**What it includes:** pricing ambition, problem statement, constraints, role of pricing  
**Typical formats:** objective page, issue tree, pricing frame

### 2) Current-state and benchmark perspective — 1–3 pages
**What it is:** How current pricing performs and how the market prices  
**What it includes:** current prices, discounting, benchmarks, value gaps, customer reactions  
**Typical formats:** benchmark table, price ladder, comparison chart

### 3) Pricing architecture — 1–2 pages
**What it is:** The structural logic of the pricing model  
**What it includes:** units, tiers, bundles, fences, segmentation, pricing logic  
**Typical formats:** tier table, architecture page, package ladder

### 4) Price levels and mechanics — 1–3 pages
**What it is:** What the price should look like in practice  
**What it includes:** price points, surcharge / discount rules, monetization mechanics, scenarios  
**Typical formats:** waterfall, value-price matrix, scenario table

### 5) Monetization implications — 1–2 pages
**What it is:** How pricing changes the economics  
**What it includes:** revenue impact, mix shifts, profitability implications, trade-offs  
**Typical formats:** value bridge, sensitivity table, economics summary

### 6) Rollout and guardrails — 1–2 pages
**What it is:** How the price model is implemented and governed  
**What it includes:** rollout logic, approval rules, KPI guardrails, governance  
**Typical formats:** rollout plan, guardrail table, KPI page

## How to build
- define the objective of pricing
- diagnose current price performance and benchmarks
- design the pricing architecture
- set price logic and monetization mechanics
- test economic implications
- define rollout and governance guardrails

## Success criteria
- pricing logic is coherent
- monetization model is explicit
- economics are visible
- rollout and guardrails are practical
- recommendation is implementable

## Common failure modes
- only benchmark pricing, no strategy
- architecture unclear
- price points without logic
- economics not tested
- governance omitted
