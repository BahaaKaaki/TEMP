# Sector strategy

## When to use this skill
Use for sector development agendas, cluster strategies, national or regional sector plans, ecosystem growth programs, or sector repositioning. Do not use for a single company strategy unless the main question is ecosystem-wide.

## Executive summary
**One page only.**  
It should track the full deck and usually contain 3–5 components such as sector mandate, baseline and gaps, strategic direction, priority interventions, and implementation direction.

## Typical output
Typical length: 12–18 pages in a standard version. This is the default for a broad ask; narrower requests may cover only one section or page, while deeper asks may extend the deck.

### 0) Executive summary — 1 page
**What it is:** One-page storyline tracker for the sector deck  
**What it includes:** sector answer, 3–5 messages, impact implication, execution direction  
**Typical formats:** message page; pillar summary; sector strategy house

### 1) Mandate and sector context — 1–2 pages
**What it is:** Why the sector matters and what the strategy is solving  
**What it includes:** mandate, ambition, boundaries, strategic context  
**Typical formats:** context page; ambition page; strategy house

### 2) Sector baseline — 2–4 pages
**What it is:** Current sector state and key bottlenecks  
**What it includes:** demand, supply, value chain, competitiveness, ecosystem, key pain points  
**Typical formats:** sector map; value-chain view; issue tree

### 3) Benchmark and outside-in perspective — 1–3 pages
**What it is:** Lessons from peer markets or leading sectors  
**What it includes:** benchmark models, comparative performance, structural lessons  
**Typical formats:** benchmark table; peer comparison; archetype view

### 4) Strategic direction — 1–3 pages
**What it is:** How the sector should evolve  
**What it includes:** target position, strategic pillars, design principles, priorities  
**Typical formats:** pillar page; strategy house; target-state view

### 5) Priority interventions — 2–4 pages
**What it is:** What must be done to shift the sector  
**What it includes:** policy levers, investment priorities, enablers, institutional actions, capability build  
**Typical formats:** initiative tree; intervention portfolio; heatmap

### 6) Delivery model and roadmap — 1–2 pages
**What it is:** How the strategy will be delivered  
**What it includes:** owners, sequencing, workstreams, milestones  
**Typical formats:** roadmap; phased plan; chevron with sub-steps

### 7) KPIs and governance — 1–2 pages
**What it is:** How progress will be tracked  
**What it includes:** sector KPIs, governance, review rhythm  
**Typical formats:** KPI scorecard; governance chart; cadence view

## How to build
- clarify mandate, ambition, and boundaries of the sector
- build the sector baseline across demand, supply, ecosystem, and policy context
- benchmark relevant peer sectors or jurisdictions
- identify the structural gaps and bottlenecks
- define strategic direction and priority intervention areas
- translate into initiatives, delivery model, and KPIs

## Typical data needed
sector size and growth; value-chain structure; policy and regulatory context; ecosystem map; benchmark cases; investment and competitiveness data; capability and infrastructure gaps; stakeholder inputs

## Success criteria
Sector ambition is clear; diagnosis is ecosystem-wide; interventions are prioritized; delivery and ownership are visible; KPIs are outcome-oriented.

## Common failure modes
Too descriptive; no real sector choices; no ecosystem logic; no link between interventions and outcomes; weak implementation ownership.
