# Team structure and QA / governance

## When to use this skill
Use when the ask is for the team page, staffing model, governance, QA, escalation logic, or joint working model in a proposal or activation document. This skill is for showing how the project will run, not for writing bios or credentials pages. Do not use for a full proposal or for a generic organization chart with no delivery logic.

## Executive summary
**One page only.**  
It should track the delivery model and usually contain 3–5 components such as coverage model, governance forums, QA flow, escalation, and joint working rhythm.

## Typical output
Typical length: 2–4 pages in a standard version. This is the default structure for a broad ask; narrower asks may be a single page, while more complex engagements may need an added resourcing or governance detail page.

### 0) Executive summary — 1 pages
**What it is:** One-page tracker for the delivery model  
**What it includes:** coverage, governance, QA, escalation, joint working  
**Typical formats:** message page; governance summary

### 1) Delivery model and coverage — 1 pages
**What it is:** How the team is structured against the scope  
**What it includes:** leadership, workstream ownership, delivery bench, specialist inputs  
**Typical formats:** org chart; workstream-to-role map

### 2) Roles and accountability — 1 pages
**What it is:** Who is responsible for what  
**What it includes:** role definitions, ownership, accountabilities, senior involvement  
**Typical formats:** RACI; accountability matrix; team pyramid

### 3) Governance forums and cadence — 1 pages
**What it is:** Where decisions and reviews happen  
**What it includes:** SteerCo, working team, PMO, workshop cadence, reporting rhythm  
**Typical formats:** governance table; meeting cadence

### 4) QA and sign-off flow — 1 pages
**What it is:** How outputs are reviewed before they reach the client  
**What it includes:** review layers, draft flow, challenge points, partner sign-off  
**Typical formats:** stage gate; QA flow; review ladder

### 5) Escalation and ways of working — 1 pages
**What it is:** How issues are handled and how the joint team operates  
**What it includes:** issue escalation, client interfaces, knowledge transfer, co-creation model  
**Typical formats:** escalation flow; joint team model

## How to build
- show how the team covers the scope rather than just listing people
- make seniority, workstream ownership, and day-to-day accountability explicit
- define governance forums, cadence, decision rights, and escalation clearly
- include a visible QA flow with review layers and sign-off logic
- show how the client team and advisor team will work together
- keep CV detail in backup and keep the core story on delivery confidence
- make the model proportionate to the scope and timeline

## Success criteria
The page explains who does what, how the work is governed, how quality is assured, and how issues are escalated, without relying on biography-heavy content.

## Common failure modes
Team page is a set of names with no coverage logic; governance is vague; QA is generic; no escalation or decision rights; the joint working model is missing or unrealistic.
