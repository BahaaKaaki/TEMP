# Workshop design and facilitation

## When to use this skill
Use when the engagement depends on one or more workshops for alignment, ideation, prioritization, or decision-making. Do not use for a broader stakeholder plan unless the main output is the workshop design itself.

## Executive summary
**One page only.**  
It should track the full deck and usually contain 3–5 components such as workshop objective, participant design, agenda and flow, facilitation model, and follow-through direction.

## Typical output
Typical length: 6–10 pages in a standard version. This is the default for a broad ask; narrower requests may cover only one section or page, while deeper asks may extend the deck.

### 0) Executive summary — 1 page
**What it is:** One-page storyline tracker for the workshop deck  
**What it includes:** workshop answer, 3–5 messages, output implication, execution direction  
**Typical formats:** message page; workshop framework; agenda summary

### 1) Objective and expected outputs — 1 page
**What it is:** What the workshop is for  
**What it includes:** decision objective, expected outputs, success definition  
**Typical formats:** objective page; output framework; issue tree

### 2) Participant design and roles — 1–2 pages
**What it is:** Who should be in the room and why  
**What it includes:** participants, roles, breakout groups, sponsors, facilitators  
**Typical formats:** stakeholder map; role table; team structure

### 3) Agenda and session flow — 1–3 pages
**What it is:** How the workshop will run  
**What it includes:** modules, timings, outputs by module, discussion logic  
**Typical formats:** agenda table; chevron with sub-steps; session flow

### 4) Facilitation approach and tools — 1–2 pages
**What it is:** How discussions will be managed  
**What it includes:** facilitation techniques, breakout logic, voting, prioritization, synthesis tools  
**Typical formats:** facilitation model; 2x2; prioritization matrix

### 5) Pre-read, preparation, and logistics — 1 page
**What it is:** What must happen before the workshop  
**What it includes:** pre-read, pre-work, room or virtual setup, logistics  
**Typical formats:** checklist; preparation plan; timeline

### 6) Capture and decision logging — 1 page
**What it is:** How outputs will be recorded  
**What it includes:** decision log, action capture, notes structure, ownership  
**Typical formats:** decision log; action tracker; meeting-output template

### 7) Follow-up and next steps — 1 page
**What it is:** How workshop outputs will be translated into action  
**What it includes:** post-workshop actions, rework, socialization, governance handoff  
**Typical formats:** roadmap; action plan; follow-up tracker

## How to build
- define the workshop objective, outputs, and decision need
- design the participant mix and roles in the room
- shape the agenda into clear modules with outputs
- define facilitation methods, pre-read, and logistics
- plan capture, decision logging, and post-workshop actions

## Success criteria
The workshop has a clear objective, the agenda is sequenced around outputs, facilitation supports decision-making, and follow-through is built in from the start.

## Common failure modes
Workshop as a discussion session; no output design; wrong participants; overloaded agenda; no pre-read; no capture or follow-up plan.
