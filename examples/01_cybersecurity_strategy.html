Recommendations

Immediate actions to accelerate Zero Trust maturity

Priority Recommendations

1

Deploy Multi-Factor Authentication Enterprise-Wide

Require MFA for all users, devices, and applications within 90 days. Prioritize privileged accounts and external-facing systems.

2

Implement Network Micro-Segmentation

Isolate critical assets and limit lateral movement. Start with crown jewel systems and expand segmentation progressively.

3

Establish 24/7 Security Operations Center

Deploy SIEM/SOAR capabilities with automated response. Consider managed services to address talent gaps.

4

Execute Board-Level Security Governance

Elevate cybersecurity to board agenda with quarterly risk reviews and investment oversight.
