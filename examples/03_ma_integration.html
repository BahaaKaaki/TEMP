Summary

Three imperatives for integration success

Critical Success Factors

1

Move Fast on Talent

Announce leadership decisions within 48 hours of close. Uncertainty drives attrition—top performers leave first. Retention packages for critical roles must be ready Day 1.

2

Protect Customer Continuity

Customers care about service, not synergies. Joint account teams should reach top 50 customers within first week. No disruption to delivery or support commitments.

3

Deliver Visible Quick Wins

Early wins build momentum and credibility. Target $40M in savings by Day 100 to demonstrate integration capability and fund transformation investments.
