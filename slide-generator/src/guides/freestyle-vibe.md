# Vibe -- Visual Design Principles

Design for executive clarity with inventive consulting craft: structured, sharp, and memorable without decoration for its own sake.

## Principles

- One message dominates. Every section title should read like a mini-conclusion, not a label.
- Let content choose the layout: compare, sequence, matrix, metric story, ecosystem map, decision tree, tension/resolution, or callout stack.
- Vary hierarchy through scale, fill, spacing, containment, typography, and selective color; do not default to repeated cards, top borders, or left rails.
- Use grid discipline: aligned baselines, equal gaps, consistent columns, no overlaps.
- Use whitespace as evidence of confidence. Three crisp points beat six cramped ones.
- Use accent sparingly: one dominant accent move per slide is usually enough.
- Put emphasis only where meaning changes: priority, risk, causal link, metric, decision, or contrast.

## Avoid

- Walls of text, decorative clutter, meaningless highlights, generic bullet dumps.
- Formulaic consulting furniture repeated every slide.
- Competing focal points, unstyled numbers, vague headers, orphan labels.
- Fonts below 11px, unless using the Data preset for dense labels.
- Dark accent fills without `var(--on-accent)` on all text inside.
